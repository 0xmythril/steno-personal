# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person keeping their own conversations on a machine they control, reading
those conversations in the portal or letting their agents search the archive.
Settings is used on both a computer and a phone.

## Product Purpose

Steno Personal archives conversations and makes them readable by the person who
could already see them and by their agents. The default experience focuses on
reading. Advanced mode reveals controls for agents to store conversations.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, exact, trustworthy. This interface holds private conversations; its voice
is plain and specific, never promotional, playful, or loud.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, gradients, promotional panels, and compose controls
in transcripts. Follow the established steno-pad system in DESIGN.md.

## Design Principles

- Keep reading conversations central; disclose advanced controls progressively.
- Explain consequences before the user commits to a destructive action.
- Keep secrets out of URLs and logs.
- Preserve the distinction between importing an archive and sending messages to
  a connected account. Steno never sends messages to those accounts.
- Keep the four-page navigation and the single-user model.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use labelled keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, and native
modal focus handling. Preserve the contrast requirements, mobile containment,
and reduced-motion behavior documented in DESIGN.md.
